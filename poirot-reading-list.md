# The Hercule Poirot Reading List

## The Guide

The Poirot stories can be read in any order, but the official guide flags some connections for spoiler-free reading.

- Read **Curtain: Poirot's Last Case** last if you intend to read all the stories.
- If you only read ten Poirot stories in your life, make sure **Curtain** is one of them.
- [Personal note] Read the **Murder of Roger Ackroyd** after you've read some other stories, so you are used to the style and methods of Poirot's investigations.

| Read first | Then read | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| **Five Little Pigs**, **Hallowe'en Party**, **Mrs McGinty's Dead** | **Elephants Can Remember** | It references plots from all three. |
| **Hallowe'en Party** | **Cat Among the Pigeons** | It namechecks a character from *Hallowe'en Party*. |
| **Mrs McGinty's Dead** | **Hallowe'en Party** | The plot of *Mrs McGinty's Dead* is discussed in *Hallowe'en Party*. |
| **Murder on the Orient Express** | **Cards on the Table** | Read *Murder on the Orient Express* first to avoid a spoiler. |
| **Three Act Tragedy** | **Hercule Poirot's Christmas** | *Hercule Poirot's Christmas* contains a spoiler from *Three Act Tragedy*. |

## Top 10 Poirot novels

1. **The Mysterious Affair at Styles** (1920)
2. **The Murder of Roger Ackroyd** (1926)
3. **Murder on the Orient Express** (1934)
4. **The ABC Murders** (1936)
5. **Death on the Nile** (1937)
6. **Hercule Poirot's Christmas** (1938)
7. **Evil Under the Sun** (1941)
8. **Five Little Pigs** (1942)
9. **After the Funeral** (1953)
10. **Curtain: Poirot's Last Case** (1975)
