# The Miss Marple Reading List

## The Guide: notes on the stories

The Miss Marple stories can be read in any order. A few placements in the chronological list below need context:

- **Sleeping Murder** was published in 1976, but written during World War II; it portrays a sprightlier Miss Marple than **Nemesis**.
- The title **Miss Marple's Final Cases** is a misnomer, because most of the short stories are actually set (and were written) in the 1940s.
- **'Greenshaw's Folly'** is published in **The Adventure of the Christmas Pudding**.

## Top 5 Miss Marple novels

In the order shown in the official guide:

1. **The Body in the Library** (1942)
2. **The Moving Finger** (1942)
3. **Sleeping Murder** (1976)
4. **A Murder Is Announced** (1950)
5. **A Pocket Full of Rye** (1953)
